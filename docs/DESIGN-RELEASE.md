# Canary experience release — 27 October 2026

The public page and application share an obsidian, warm ivory and restrained gold identity. The reporting use case leads: keep a dossier private while checking in, and prepare a chosen editor for conditional recovery. The interactive Maya/Noor example remains real local cryptography with simulated chain and keeper records.

## Original artwork

`public/assets/canary-vault.webp` and `docs/assets/canary-vault.webp` are the same original AI-generated concept illustration, created with the built-in image-generation tool. The 1672 × 941 PNG was converted to WebP at quality 87 (83,090 bytes), without compositional edits. It is an illustration, not a protocol state or a substitute for the separate interactive LivingVault.

Generation brief: Create an original premium cinematic website hero for Canary, an encrypted reporting dossier. Place a slim standing charcoal folder with visible ivory paper edges inside smoked glass with a restrained brass clasp, on an obsidian stone stage. Use precise warm lighting from the upper right and a fine gold handoff line. Keep the left 48 percent dark and quiet for live typography. Use tactile materials and architectural composition. No people, coins, birds, padlocks, text, logos, neon or generic glowing interface elements.

References were studied for principles, not copied assets or code: [Kage](https://kage-landing.vercel.app/), [Xorr](https://github.com/nickthelegend/xorr-solana) and [GHOST](https://github.com/nickthelegend/ghost-nebius). The useful principles were a single product centerpiece, a direct outcome headline, restrained motion, and consistent participant/status semantics.
