Prototype boundaries & trust model Read technical scope +

Hosted browser lab. Encryption, signatures, threshold reconstruction, recipient-key decryption and digest checks are real. Its blocks, keeper records and confirmations are a resettable simulation.

External wallet handoff. The hosted app can prepare a ZIP-321 testnet REGISTER request and open a supplied txid in the explorer; it does not independently query lightwalletd or label that tx confirmed.

Native proof. Real testnet transactions and five durable keeper processes were exercised. Those processes currently share one operator and scanner endpoint, not five independent organizations.

Security status. Testnet prototype, not audited. Three colluding keepers could release early to the designated recipient; delivered shares cannot be recalled. Canary does not determine whether someone is safe or guarantee anonymity.
