Know the trust boundaries +

A tested prototype, not an audited tool for sensitive reporting. Use the harmless public fixture. Five native keeper processes currently share one operator and scanner. Three colluding keepers could release early to the designated recipient, and delivered shares cannot be recalled.

Canary does not determine whether someone is safe, automatically publish a story, or guarantee anonymity. Network observers and lightwallet operators can learn metadata. No smart contract is deployed. Native wallet operations require a local runtime; the hosted app does not connect one automatically.
